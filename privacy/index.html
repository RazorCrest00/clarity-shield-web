What stays in this tab

  • Messages, selected files, reports, and next steps stay in this tab.

  • Refreshing, closing the tab, or choosing “Clear session” clears them.

Microphone use

  • The microphone starts only after you give permission and choose “Start listening.”

  • Choosing “Stop listening” turns the microphone off.

  • Clarity Shield cannot hear a phone call directly. Use speakerphone or a second device, and get consent when required by law.
